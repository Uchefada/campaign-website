About the candidate

Nnadozie Chikezie

Educational, professional and community-service profile.

Educational & Professional Background

His educational journey began at St. John Primary School, Naze, and St. Theresa Primary School, Nekede. He subsequently attended Nekede Secondary School and Bishop Shanahan College, Orlu, before proceeding to the Federal University of Technology, Owerri (FUTO).

After graduating from FUTO, he worked as a lecturer at the Federal College of Education (Technical), Umunze. He later moved to the United Kingdom and obtained a master’s degree from the University of Aberdeen.

He currently resides and works in the United Kingdom, where he is involved in educational services, staff recruitment and other business activities.

Human-Capacity Development

While living abroad, Nnadozie Chikezie has continued to maintain close connections with his people at home and in the diaspora.

Through his educational, employment and human-capacity development activities, he has reportedly assisted more than 200 people with opportunities to relocate to the United Kingdom to live and work. More than 50 beneficiaries are from Naze community, with others coming from communities across Owerri Federal Constituency and beyond.

Community Interventions

His community-development activities have included educational assistance through payment of WAEC registration and examination fees, scholarship support for nursing students studying in Turkey, assistance with hospital bills and support for health-insurance initiatives for children under five years old.

He has also contributed to road maintenance in Naze and Nekede, supported street-light projects, assisted small-scale businesses and helped members of the diaspora community, particularly those living in the United Kingdom to access employment opportunities.

A Record of Community Service

His professional background in education and his involvement in human-capacity development, healthcare support, infrastructure, employment and entrepreneurship form the foundation of his public-service profile as he seeks to represent Owerri Federal Constituency in the Federal House of Representatives in 2027.
